# Privacy Policy for Tatscale

**Last updated:** September 17, 2026

Tatscale ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by Tatscale ("the developer").
This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your
choices. We keep this deliberately short and plain.

## Summary

- You can use the app as a **guest** without giving us any personal data.
- If you sign in, we store your **email address**, your **name** if you share it,
  an **account ID**, and a **count of how many exports** you have used.
- You sign in with **Sign in with Apple** or a **one-time code** sent to your email.
- If you subscribe, **Apple** tells us about events such as renewals and offer
  codes used, but never your payment details.
- We **do not** sell your data, show third-party ads, use analytics or tracking
  tools, or track you across other apps or websites.
- Your stencils, designs, and print settings stay **on your device**.

## What we collect

**Account information.**
If you sign in, we store:
- Your **email address** — either one you type in, or the email provided by
  Sign in with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email,"
  we only receive Apple's private relay address.
- Your **name**, if you share it (for example through Sign in with Apple).
- A unique **account identifier** used to recognise you across sessions and devices.

**Export count.**
We store the number of exports you have used, so the free trial works across
reinstalls and devices. We store only the number, never the files you export.

**Sign in with Apple token.**
If you sign in with Apple, we store a token issued by Apple. It is used only to
disconnect Tatscale from your Apple Account when you delete your account.

You can use the app as a **guest** without providing any of this. Guests can
design freely but need to sign in to export or print.

**Purchase information.**
If you subscribe, the transaction is handled entirely by **Apple**. We do not
see or store your payment details, and your subscription status is checked with
Apple on your device.

Apple also notifies our server when something happens to a subscription — for
example when it starts, renews or ends, or when an offer code is redeemed. Each
notice includes details such as the plan, any offer code used, the dates, the
price and currency, the App Store country, whether it will renew, and Apple's
transaction reference. It does not include your name, email address or payment
details, and we do not link it to your Tatscale account. We keep these records
to see which offer codes, such as those shared by partner tattoo artists, lead
to subscriptions.

**Your designs stay local.**
Imported images, stencils, guides, grids, and print/export settings are stored
**on your device only**. We do not upload, view, or store your artwork.

## How we use your information

- To provide account login and keep you signed in.
- To run the free trial by counting your exports.
- To see which offer codes lead to subscriptions and renewals, for example codes
  shared by partner tattoo artists.
- To send you product updates, tips, and offers **if you opted in** by providing
  your email. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Who we share it with

We use the following service providers to operate the app:

- **Apple** — for Sign in with Apple, subscription billing, and notices about
  subscription events.
- **Supabase** — to securely store your account information, export count, and
  subscription records.
- **Resend** — to deliver the one-time sign-in codes we email you.

These providers process data on our behalf. We do **not** sell your personal
information to anyone.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the menu (top left),
go to your account pane, and tap **Delete account**. This permanently deletes
your email, name, account ID and export count, and disconnects Sign in with
Apple. You can also request deletion by emailing us (see below).

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are
managed by Apple in Settings.

Subscription records are not linked to your account, so deleting your account
does not remove them. We keep them only as long as we need them to measure how
offer codes perform.

## Your choices

- **Use as a guest** — provide no personal data at all.
- **Unsubscribe** from emails at any time.
- **Delete your account** in the app, or request deletion by emailing us.

## Children

Tatscale is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top
reflects the latest version.
